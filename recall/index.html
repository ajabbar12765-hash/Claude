Photosynthesis
Plants convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. It happens inside chloroplasts, in two linked stages.
6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
Light reactions
In the thylakoids. Light splits water, releasing O₂ and making ATP and NADPH.
Calvin cycle
In the stroma. ATP and NADPH power CO₂ fixation into glucose.
Key term — Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light.
