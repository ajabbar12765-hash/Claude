Games for a tall screen

Each game is built around one-thumb taps on a 192 × 490 screen. Your best scores stay in this browser.

Put a wallpaper on your Band 9

  1. Save a PNG from Wallpapers or AI Wallpapers. Choose Native 192 × 490 so every pixel maps 1:1 to the screen.
  2. On your Android phone, open Mi Fitness and tap your band.
  3. Open Watch faces (Band displays), go to the online gallery and add a Photo watch face.
  4. Open that face's edit screen, choose Background or Photos, and pick your saved image. Mi Fitness lets you add several photos to cycle through.
  5. Choose the clock style and position, then tap Apply. The band syncs in a few seconds.
Tip: use the Clock preview under the band to check the time stays readable where you plan to place it. Mi Fitness puts the clock on top of your photo.

Why native size looks sharpest

The Band 9 screen is 192 pixels wide and 490 tall. A larger image gets shrunk by Mi Fitness, which softens fine lines. Every wallpaper here is drawn as vector shapes straight onto the 192 × 490 grid, so edges land cleanly on real pixels. The 2× option is there if you want a copy for your phone or for sharing.

Games on the band itself

Right now the games run here, in a band-sized screen you can play on your phone. Running them on the Band 9 itself takes two extra steps:

  • Each game has to be rebuilt as a Xiaomi Vela quick app (an .rpk file). The Band 9 can run these.
  • Xiaomi's store doesn't take personal apps, so you'd sideload the .rpk onto the band from your Android phone with a community tool such as AstroBox.
Sideloading is unofficial. Keep Mi Fitness up to date, install only files you trust, and expect that a band firmware update may remove sideloaded apps.
