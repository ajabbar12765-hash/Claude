Before you ask

How long does it really take?

Four weeks for a brand site, about ten days for a single landing page, roughly six for a store — counted from the discovery call, not from the deposit. The clock pauses if we're waiting on you for photos or feedback, which is the only thing that usually slips a date.

What if I don't like the design?

You see a real clickable design in week one, before any of it is built. Two rounds of revisions are included at that stage, because changing a design is cheap and changing a finished site is not. That is deliberately the point where we get it right — the booking deposit holds a slot someone else could have taken, so it isn't refundable.

Do I have to write the copy?

No. You answer questions on the discovery call and we write it. If you'd rather write it yourself, that's fine too — just get it to us before the build week.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Yes. At handover you get a recorded walkthrough showing how to change text, swap images and add pages. No monthly fee, no permission needed, no calling us to change a phone number.

What happens after launch?

Thirty days of fixes included, free — anything broken, anything wrong, anything you spot late. After that you can keep it yourself or ask us about ongoing care, but nothing is compulsory.

How does payment work?

Half to book your slot in the calendar, half on launch day. The booking half is non-refundable — it takes a date off the calendar. The price is fixed in writing before anything starts, so the number you agree is the number you pay.

Who will I actually be working with?

The designer who builds your site, directly. No account manager, no handover to a junior, no ticket queue. That's also why the number of slots is capped.
