Before you order

A few questions

How much notice do you need?

Orders need 48 hours' notice — everything is baked to order, so nothing is sitting ready-made on a shelf.

Where do you deliver?

Across Karachi. Message us your area on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.

Can I mix flavours in one box?

Yes — every box is split evenly between any two flavours from the tier you're ordering. Tell us which two when you message us.

How do I actually place an order?

Tap any "Order" button on this page — it opens WhatsApp with your flavours or box already written in. Send it and we'll confirm the details with you directly.

Something else?

Message us on WhatsApp — customisations, bulk orders, anything not covered here gets sorted out directly.
