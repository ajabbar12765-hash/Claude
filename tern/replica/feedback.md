## What they hate (unverified)
1. Limits that change and are not documented
2. Paying and still hitting caps; upsells at the cap
3. Surprise billing

## What is missing (unverified)
1. Event-triggered automations (only on the paid tier, if at all)
2. Reliable multi-step work across apps

## What is unsolved (unverified)
1. Seeing what the assistant actually did in your accounts before trusting it with more
