Our story

Made the way you imagined it — or better

Riti Boutique began as a small atelier in Dubai and grew on word of mouth alone. Customers bring us a photo, a memory, a fabric they have carried across a continent. We take the measurements, cut the pattern and hand-finish the work in our own studio in Al Karama.

Bridal lehengas, Onam and festive collections, blouses stitched to the millimetre, and one-off pieces designed around you. If you are outside the UAE, send your measurements online — we have shipped to customers in Australia, India and Nepal without a single fitting.

  • Fully customised design, colour and fit
  • In-house stitching & hand work
  • Deadline work handled — including last-minute bridal
  • Online measurement service for overseas customers

How it works

Four steps, one fitting

  1. 1

    Message us

    Send a photo of what you have in mind on WhatsApp, with your date. We reply with what is possible and what it will cost.

  2. 2

    Design & measure

    Visit the Karama studio, or send measurements online if you are overseas. We advise on fabric, colour and cut.

  3. 3

    We stitch it

    Cut, stitched and hand-finished in house. You get progress updates — no silence until collection day.

  4. 4

    Fit & collect

    One fitting, adjustments the same week, then collect in Karama or we ship it to you.
