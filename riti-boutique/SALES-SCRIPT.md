## 2. Before you send anything

- **Demo is live:** https://riti-boutique.vercel.app — already deployed, already linked into every message below. Open it once yourself before sending anything.
- **Message from a real, warmed-up number**, not a fresh SIM. UAE telecom rules and WhatsApp's own Business Policy both treat unsolicited bulk messaging as spam — a cold number that blasts templates gets reported and banned fast. One personal, clearly-relevant message to one business is a different thing, and reads like one.
- **Send Tue–Sun, 10:30–12:30 or 18:00–20:30 Gulf time.** Inside their opening hours, away from the rush. **Never Monday** — they are closed.
- **Ask for Aparna by name.** She is the person customers thank in review after review. If she is the owner, you are talking to the decision-maker; if not, "Aparna" still proves you did homework.
- The owners are Malayali (Onam collections, Kerala clientele). A single Malayalam word — *namaskaram* — as a greeting is a warm touch if you speak it. Do not fake a whole message in it.

## 6. About the AED 265 price — read this before you send it

You asked for 20,000 PKR converted. At the 11 Aug 2026 rate (1 PKR = 0.01322 AED) that is **AED 264.35** — call it AED 265.

That number is correct. It is also, in my honest read, working against you in this specific market:

- **It undercuts their own product by a factor of five.** One of their own reviewers described a **AED 400 budget for stitching a single dress.** You would be pricing an entire website below one blouse. To a business owner, that does not read as a bargain — it reads as "this cannot be real."
- **Dubai's floor for a small-business site is roughly AED 1,500–3,500.** AED 265 sits so far under it that the natural conclusion is a template mill or a scam.
- **They are a premium brand.** 4.9 stars, bridal work, seven years, celebrity-feel reviews. Cheap suppliers are a risk to that, not a saving.
- **You already spent the money.** The demo is built. Discounting it to AED 265 gives away the strongest proof-of-work asset you have.

**My recommendation:** quote **AED 1,850**. Same script, same demo, one number changed. If you want the low price as a door-opener, make it a reason rather than a level — *"AED 265 for the first three boutiques I do in Karama, because I want the portfolio pieces and the referrals"* — so the discount attaches to a story instead of to your value.

Your call. Both messages are written above, ready to send.
